Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

"n8n homelab" (shown on Google's consent screen as "tracker") is a personal automation used by one person: its owner. It runs on the owner's own server and is not offered to anyone else.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app can read messages in the owner's Gmail account (including headers, body and attachments of the emails it inspects) and add labels to messages. It also sees the owner's Google account email address and basic profile information that Google provides at sign-in.

Why

Only to find statement emails from banks and card issuers and tag them with the Gmail labels "Statements" and "Statements/Processed". Nothing else.

Where the data goes

  • Data is processed on the owner's own server, in the owner's private home network.
  • It is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train any machine-learning or AI model.
  • No human other than the owner reads the data.

Storage and retention

The automation keeps run history on the owner's server for a limited time (about 14 days by default), after which it is deleted automatically. The labels added to emails remain in the owner's Gmail account until the owner removes them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete stored run history on the server at any time.
