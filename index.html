n8n homelab

This is a small, private automation that runs on the owner's own home server. It connects to the owner's own Gmail account (through Google's official sign-in and Gmail API) to find bank and card statement emails and tag them with a Gmail label, so they are easy to find and can be organized later.

It is not a public service. There are no other users, no accounts, and no sign-up. The only person who signs in to this app, and the only person whose data it can reach, is its owner.

What it does

  • Looks for statement emails in the owner's mailbox.
  • Adds the Gmail labels Statements and Statements/Processed.
  • Does not delete, send, forward, or modify the content of any email.
